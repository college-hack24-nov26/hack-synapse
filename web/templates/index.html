Terms of Clinical & Investigational Use

1. Investigational Device Software Exemption

The NeuroGlove Multimodal Edge-AI Rehabilitation System is provided for clinical investigation, neuromuscular rehabilitation research, and qualified physiological monitoring under institutional clinical protocols compliant with IEC 62304 / ISO 13485 guidelines.

2. Professional Clinical Oversight

All motor training sessions, range-of-motion assessments, and biofeedback routines must be conducted under the direction of a certified physical therapist, occupational therapist, or licensed rehabilitation clinician. Software metrics represent assistive biomechanical estimations and do not substitute for formal diagnostic evaluations.

3. Hardware Operating Tolerances & Safety Cutoffs

Ensure all 5 flex sensor channels, MPU-6050 IMU, sEMG differential leads, and palmar FSR sensors undergo zero-point calibration before patient engagement. In the event of skin irritation, sensory fatigue, or hardware telemetry desynchronization, immediately terminate the active session and power down the glove controller.

4. Calibration & Signal Integrity

Analog-to-digital conversions (ADC) and surface electromyography envelopes are calibrated per individual subject anatomy. Data collected with uncalibrated sensors must not be utilized for longitudinal clinical progress scoring.

Patient Data Privacy & HIPAA Security Policy

1. Protected Health Information (PHI) De-Identification

In accordance with 45 CFR § 164.514 (HIPAA Privacy Rule), all kinematic session logs, flex angles, EMG root-mean-square values, and motor attempt classifications are recorded against pseudo-anonymized Session IDs. No direct patient identifiers (name, MRN, date of birth) are transmitted over unencrypted telemetry channels.

2. Local Edge Architecture & Data Locality

All machine learning inference (Random Forest classifier and EMG envelope feature extraction) operates on-premise at the edge controller (Raspberry Pi / local workstation). No biomechanical raw time-series feeds are uploaded to third-party public cloud endpoints.

3. Secure Local Transport & Logging

Telemetry between the ESP32 wearable acquisition node and the workstation broker utilizes TLS-authenticated MQTT protocols. Local SQLite history records reside solely on the physical workstation filesystem under authenticated clinical user accounts.

4. Patient Rights of Inspection & Erasure

Under clinical research standards, patients or their legal guardians may request an export or secure erasure of their rehabilitation trajectory logs at any time through the workstation administration console.
